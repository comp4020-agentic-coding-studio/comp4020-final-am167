# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?
Rereading the crit spec before building anything. My plan had the logging as totals I could use later, like launches per band and how many collisions there were. However, the spec asked for something completely different; a line for each thing a person does, saying who did it, so I could tell the story of two people using the app from the logs alone. Catching that early meant I built the right thing the first time instead of redoing it.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I didn't do much work this week, and I'm fine with that. What mattered was understanding the app's purpose clearly enough to turn down features that didn't serve it. I want to be the developer who holds the idea steady while the agent does the building, instead of letting a working feature in just because it's there.
