# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The main breakthrough was settling on an idea. I spent most of the first days
asking the agent for ideas, telling it to explore them further, and then
rejecting them. A lot of them felt too shallow, and others had requirements
that would've been hard to develop in a way that could convey the briefs
requirements. I looked through other peoples READMEs to get a sense of which
general ideas others had taken. I had asked it to try to think of something
related to assignment 1 or 2, but it was too fixated on those. Finally it
suggested something about conveying Kessler syndrome. It was the first idea
where I felt the mechanic was the argument: everyone launching to be seen is
what fills the sky with debris. Once it was chosen, everything else moved
quickly.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This constant iteration taught me that asking an agent for more ideas doesn't
make the choice easier for me. It will keep producing reasonable options
forever, but it can't tell me which one I actually care about - that part has
to come from me. I want to be a developer who owns those decisions and writes
down why, instead of one who accepts whatever the agent suggests first.
